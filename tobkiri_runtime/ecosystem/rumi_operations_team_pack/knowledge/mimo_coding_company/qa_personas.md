# QA Personas

Use these personas across browser and computer-use loops:

- `first_time_user`: looks for onboarding confusion, missing defaults, and unclear next steps.
- `power_user`: explores settings, model routing, batch actions, and advanced flows.
- `impatient_user`: clicks quickly, interrupts loading, retries buttons, and exposes race conditions.
- `keyboard_heavy_user`: tests focus order, text input, and shortcut-driven workflows.
